# Profiles

Sign in to Codebolt Cloud to view a developer’s People profile or your own user profile.

## People profiles

On an agent’s detail page, select the username beside **Created by** to open the author’s People profile. You can also open `/people/<username>` directly. The **Agents**, **MCPs**, **Templates**, and **Apps** tabs group their published work. Select an agent or MCP card to open its details. To open a template or app listing, find it in the corresponding [Registry catalog](./01_overview.md).

## Your user profile

Open `/userProfile` to see the name, email, and avatar associated with your current session. The **Update** button is disabled. To view items you’ve published, use the corresponding **My** view in the [Registry](./01_overview.md).
