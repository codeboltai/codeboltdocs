# Profiles

Codebolt Cloud has two profile surfaces:

- **People profiles** (`/people/:username`) — a **public** page for any developer, showing everything they've published.
- **Your user profile** (`/userProfile`) — your **personal** page with your own published items and account info.

## People profiles

Every developer who has published to the marketplace has a public profile at `/people/<username>`. It's a showcase of their work, organized into tabs:

| Tab | Shows |
|---|---|
| **Agents** | Agents they've authored |
| **MCPs** | MCP servers they've published |
| **Templates** | Project templates they've shared |
| **Apps** | Packaged apps they've built |

Each card links through to the full detail page for that item. Profiles are how you discover an author's broader catalog after finding one useful item — click an agent's author to see what else they've made.

## Your user profile

`/userProfile` is your personal landing page. It mirrors your public People profile (your published agents, MCPs, templates, and apps) and adds account-level context. Use it to:

- Review everything you've published at a glance
- Jump straight into editing or updating a listing
- Share your profile link so others can find your work

For editing your name, email, title, avatar, timezone, language, and sync preferences, use [Account Settings](../05_settings/10_account-settings.md).
