# Account Settings

Open `/settings` in the cloud portal to see the **Profile**, **Password**, **Notification**, **Integration**, and **Billing** tabs.

The **Profile**, **Password**, and **Notification** forms are previews. Submitting them leaves your saved profile, sign-in password, and notification preferences unchanged, even if a success message appears.

| Tab | What appears in the portal |
|---|---|
| **Profile** | Name (3–12 characters), email, avatar upload, title, language, time zone, and **Sync Data**. The language menu offers English (US), Chinese, Japanese, and French. |
| **Password** | Current password, new password, and confirmation fields. |
| **Notification** | Email, browser, and app options for news, account activity, sign-ins, reminders, mentions, replies, task updates, assignments, new products, and orders. |
| **Integration** | **Installed** and **Available** app lists. |
| **Billing** | Payment methods and billing history sections. |

To view your current account details, open [General Settings](./01_overview.md). For subscriptions and usage, see [Plans, Usage & Billing](./08_subscription-plans-usage-billing.md). For team membership and roles, see [Team Settings](./09_team-settings.md).
