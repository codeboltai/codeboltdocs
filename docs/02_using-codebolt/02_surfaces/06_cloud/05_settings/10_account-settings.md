# Account Settings

**Account Settings** (`/settings`) is the older tabbed editor for your personal account identity. It's separate from [General Settings](./01_overview.md) (which is a read-only snapshot) and is where you actually edit your details.

## The five tabs

| Tab | What you edit |
|---|---|
| **Profile** | Name, email, title, avatar, timezone, language, and a "sync data" toggle |
| **Password** | Change your password |
| **Notification** | Email/notification preferences across categories (news, account activity, sign-in, reminders, mentions, replies, task updates, assignments, new products, new orders) |
| **Integration** | Connected integrations |
| **Billing** | Billing details and history |

## Profile tab

The Profile form collects:

- **Name** — your display name (3–12 characters)
- **Email** — validated email address
- **Title** — your role or title
- **Avatar** — upload or set an image URL
- **Timezone** — your local timezone
- **Language** — UI language (English, 中文, 日本语, French)
- **Sync data** — toggle whether your usage data syncs

Changes here are what other users see on your [People profile](../03_registry/04_profiles.md).

## Notification tab

Granular control over what you're notified about, grouped by category. Toggle individual events within each group to silence or enable them — useful for keeping signal high and noise low when you have many agents and environments running.

## How this relates to the rest of Settings

- For **subscription** changes (plan, usage, billing), use [Plans, Usage & Billing](./08_subscription-plans-usage-billing.md).
- For **agent** configuration (tokens, sandboxes, providers), use the Agent Settings pages.
- For **team** configuration, use [Team Settings](./09_team-settings.md).

This page is purely your **personal identity and preferences**.
